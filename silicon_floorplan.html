Placement Strategy

The four processing elements form a symmetrical $2 \times 2$ grid with direct vertical metal interconnects (`acc_00_to_10`, `acc_01_to_11`) routed on Metal 2. The central 6-state FSM controller orchestrates control strobes (`load_weight`, `swap_weights`) with balanced wire lengths to all four corner PEs, preventing clock and control skew.
