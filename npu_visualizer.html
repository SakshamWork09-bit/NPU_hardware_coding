2. What a D-Flip-Flop (DFF) Actually Does

In Verilog, reg [7:0] is not a software variable—it generates 8 physical 1-bit D-Flip-Flops clocked to posedge clk.

D Input: 1
[ D FLIP-FLOP ]
Clock: 0
Q Output: 0

Total 263 DFFs in npu_top breakdown: 228 in PEs, 9 in Skew FIFOs, 8 in FSM Controller, 18 in Requantizers. 263 DFFs does NOT mean 263 CPU cores.
